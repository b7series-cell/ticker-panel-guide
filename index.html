Ticker panel guide

Every section of the side panel that opens when you click a row in Live watch, top to bottom. Each one has a drawing with numbered callouts and a green "before an alert" line. The thresholds come from the bot's code and your current config, so the numbers here are the ones the bot really uses.

Drawings use made-up MU numbers so the parts are easy to see. If you change a setting in the Settings tab, the panel follows the new value (the gauge line and "3 needed" are read live), but this page won't.

  2. 2The minute that is still forming. Webull refreshes it every 15 to 30 seconds, so the numbers in sections 2 and 3 jump around until the minute closes. QQQ says reference only, no alerts here. "No forming bar yet" means Webull hasn't sent this minute.

2

The five tiles

The day's context in one row. Two of these, price vs VWAP and session delta, decide which direction the bot is allowed to trade.

  1. 1Price, with above / below VWAP and the VWAP price. VWAP here already includes the forming bar, so it matches what the closed-bar check will see.
  2. 2This bar: shares traded so far in the forming minute and its delta (shares bought at the ask minus shares sold at the bid). Green when buyers lead, red when sellers do.
  3. 3Session delta: the running total of every bar's delta since 9:30, and the Nasdaq volume it is based on. Nasdaq is about a quarter of all volume, so compare it to itself, not to other tools.
  4. 4Day range: the session's low and high so far (regular hours).
  5. 5Vs today's value: is price above, inside or below today's developing value area (the 70% of volume around today's POC), with that area printed underneath.
Before an alertFor a long you want tile 1 to say above VWAP and tile 3 to be green. For a short, below VWAP and red. If they disagree, absorption (trend mode) and stacked imbalances (confirmation on) will both be skipped.
3

Setting up on the forming bar

How close each Plan 1 setup is to triggering on the minute that is still open. It never sends an alert or places an order. It's an early warning.

  1. 1Buying at the high: of all the shares bought (at the ask) in this bar, what % were bought at the bar's single highest price. The bar fills from 0 to 50%; past 50% it stays full. The black tick is the 22% line (absorption_pct). A big share at the very top means buyers hit a wall there: the setup for an absorption short.
  2. 2Selling at the low: the same thing for sellers at the bar's lowest price, the setup for an absorption long. In the drawing 31% of the bar's selling sits at the low, past the line, so a flag appears.
  3. 3Stacked imbalances buy / sell: the longest run of price rows in a row that each have an imbalance (drawing below). 3 needed (stack_min). The number turns bold once one side reaches 3.
  4. 4Divergence: shows "new 5-bar high, delta −800" (or low) when this bar has broken the last 5 bars' high or low, with its delta. A flag appears only if the delta points the other way and session delta hasn't confirmed.
  5. 5Near a level: any of your levels (section 7) within 3¢ of this bar's high-to-low range.
  6. 6Flags: a plain-English line for each setup that has reached its threshold. "(but below VWAP)" or "not at a level" at the end means it's on the wrong side and will probably be skipped at the close. With no flags it says "Nothing setting up on this bar".

Today, 1-min candles with your levels

The whole day so far, premarket included, with every level the bot uses drawn across it.

  1. 1Premarket candles (4:00–9:30) are drawn faint. They are never alerted, but their high and low become the PM high and PM low levels.
  2. 2The dashed vertical line marks the 9:30 open.
  3. 3Blue dashed lines are your levels, labelled on the right with name and price: prior-day POC / VAH / VAL (pPOC, pVAH, pVAL), PM high / low, block prints (e.g. Block 25k @ 10:14) and manual levels. Labels are nudged apart so they don't overlap.
  4. 4The dotted line is VWAP, the line in tile 1.
  5. 5▲ / ▼ mark today's alerts on this stock, at the entry price. Hover one for setup, side and result.
  6. 6The faded last candle is the minute still forming; the dark tag on the right is the last price.
  7. 7The subtitle says the time span and how many levels are in view. Levels too far from today's range are left off the chart and listed in the note underneath ("Off the chart: …"). Your screenshot showed 102 because the LVLS zones were still in; with those removed (Nov 15) you'll see a handful.
Before an alertIs the forming candle touching a dashed line (within 3¢)? Stacked and divergence alerts need that. Is it on the right side of the dotted VWAP for the direction? Absorption in trend mode ignores levels but still needs the VWAP side.
5

Footprint, latest bars

The last 10 one-minute bars, opened up: how many shares sold at the bid (left) and bought at the ask (right) at every price. It scrolls sideways and starts at the newest bar. It only appears with footprint_chart = true.

  1. 1Each cell is sold × bought at that price: left number = sellers hitting the bid, right = buyers lifting the ask. Volume is Nasdaq-only.
  2. 2Cell colour: blue when more was bought than sold at that price, orange when more was sold. Darker means more shares and a more one-sided split.
  3. 3Outlined right half, bold number = a buy imbalance: bought at this price ≥ 3× sold one row lower, and at least 200 shares. Three on top of each other (here 812.06–812.08) are a stack of 3. An outlined left half is a sell imbalance (sold ≥ 3× bought one row higher).
  4. 4▸ marks the bar's own POC, the price with the most shares.
  5. 5The thin candle at the left of each column shows open, high, low, close (blue up, orange down).
  6. 6Under each bar: total Vol and Δ delta (green positive, red negative).
  7. 7The dashed box is the bar still forming.

The note under the chart says how much price each row is. When a stock moves fast the chart groups several cents into one row so it fits. Then the outlines are worked out on the grouped rows, while the bot itself always uses single cents, so trust section 3's count over the outlines.

Before an alertStack long: three or more bold outlined right-half cells in a row, a positive Δ, and the candle closing near the top of the bar. Absorption long: a heavy left number on the bar's bottom row (lots sold at the low) with the candle closing at least 2¢ above it. Mirror both for shorts.

  1. 1POC: today's price with the most trading.
  2. 2Value area: the 70% of today's volume around the POC. Tile 5 tells you if price is above, inside or below it.
  3. 3Opening range: high and low of the first 30 minutes (Plan 2's opening range breakout).
  4. 4Initial balance: high and low of the first hour, set at 10:30.
  5. 5Opened vs prior value: where today's open sat against yesterday's value area. Opening inside and then moving through value is the 80% rule's setup.
  6. 6Prior POC / value area (letters): yesterday's numbers from the 30-minute letter profile. They can differ by a few cents from the pPOC / pVAH / pVAL levels, which come from yesterday's volume.
  7. 7Single prints: a zone traded in only one 30-minute period, a fast move that often gets revisited.
  8. 8Buying tail / Selling tail: single prints at the very bottom or top of the day, a sign the extreme was rejected. A poor high or low (two or more periods flat at the extreme, no tail) is unfinished business.
Before an alertFor Plan 2 alerts, look at where price is against the value area edges, the opening range and any single prints. Plan 2 stops for the day after 3 half-hour closes across the developing POC (choppy day).

Today's alerts

Every alert on this stock today, in the order they fired.

  1. 1Time of the signal bar (ET), setup and side.
  2. 2Entry, stop and target (T1) prices the alert gave.
  3. 3Result (open, pending, T1 then BE, stopped, expired and so on) and R: how many risk units it made or lost. +1R = made what was risked.
  4. 4Click a row to open the alert detail panel: a chart around the alert with entry, stop and targets drawn, the delta of each minute, and a "Why it fired" table with what the bot saw and which levels it touched.
Before an alertCount the rows still open or pending across the plan (not only this stock). Plan 1 allows 2 open at once; a third alert is blocked, and the Dashboard shows why.
